# r-package-umet

## Title

Utility-Based Dose Optimization Approach for Multiple-Dose Randomized Trial Designs (U-MET)

## Description

U-MET compares randomized doses with a utility-based approach accounting for safety and
efficacy. The approach compares two randomized doses by extending the BOIN12 approach to use the
standardized utility scores in a hypothesis testing framework, where a frequentist and Bayesian inference
are evaluated to compare the mean utilities across doses and identify the OBD.
